# Contributing

Bug reports, benchmarks and pull requests are welcome.

- **Seek or speed changes:** include before and after numbers from several runs.
  Seek medians on real swarms vary by at least 25% between runs.
- **Tests:** add one for each bug you fix. CI runs `ziyron-meta` and `ziyron-cli`,
  and compiles everything else. Getting the full suite into CI is on the roadmap.
- **Test torrents:** use only content you may share, such as Sintel or Big Buck Bunny.
- **Licence:** contributions are accepted under MPL-2.0.
